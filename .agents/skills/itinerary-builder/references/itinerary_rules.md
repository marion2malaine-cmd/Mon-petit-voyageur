# Itinerary Rules

## Pacing guards

- Max 2 major activities per day, plus free visits that sit on the way.
- Never two cross-region transfers on back-to-back days.
- One lighter day every 3-4 days.
- With children or a slow pace: 1h30 of driving each way maximum, and a water
  or shade break in the afternoon.
- Every day has one backup option for weather or fatigue.
- Meals are part of the timeline, not an afterthought: lunch between 12h and
  14h, dinner from 19h30.

## Variety matrix

Track these four axes and make sure no two days repeat the same combination:

| Axis | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | archaeology, old town, mountains, sea, gastronomy, crafts, wildlife |
| Landscape | coast, plateau, gorge, plain, island, city |
| Effort | slow, walking, hiking, driving day, boat |
| Moment | market morning, sunset viewpoint, evening festival, night stroll |

## Self-check before answering

Run this checklist and fix anything that fails:

1. Are all day themes distinct? Are all `area` values distinct or clearly
   justified?
2. Does every day have at least 3 free visits, and is **every free visit name
   unique across the whole trip**?
3. Does every day have 2-3 paid options that are alternatives, not a sequence?
4. Is every restaurant name unique across the trip? Does each day have a lunch
   and a dinner option?
5. Are all `booking_links` empty and all `map_url` null? (The app builds them.)
6. Do all photo fields contain only a `query`?
7. Does any sentence repeat from another day? Rewrite it.
8. Are the clock times chronological and realistic given the travel times?
