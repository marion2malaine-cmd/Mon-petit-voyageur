# Free Culture Playbook

Where to find genuinely free cultural visits in any destination. Use this to
reach the maximum number of distinct free visits, with a different mix each day.

## Always free, everywhere

- Historic centres, old towns, medinas, venetian or colonial quarters
- Harbours, fishing ports, fortification walkways, lighthouses seen from outside
- Churches, cathedrals, monasteries, mosques and temples with free access
- Cemeteries of historic or artistic interest
- Covered and open-air markets; artisan streets and workshops seen from the door
- Public squares, fountains, arcades, staircases, panoramic roads
- Viewpoints, belvederes, sunset spots
- Public beaches, lakes, riverbanks, coastal paths
- Public gardens, botanical parks with free entry, arboretums
- Street art districts and open-air murals
- Village life: bakeries, festivals, evening promenades, religious processions
- Archaeological remains visible from public paths and fenced sites seen from
  outside
- University campuses, libraries and covered galleries open to the public
- Free-entry days: many national museums are free on a set day each month, and
  most sites are free for EU residents under 18 or under 25 — always state the
  condition in `free_note` rather than claiming plain "free".

## Rules for choosing

- Name the exact place. "A pretty church" is worthless; "Agios Titos church in
  Heraklion" is a visit.
- Put the free visit on the day's route, not 40 km away.
- Mix categories inside a day: one monument + one market + one viewpoint beats
  three churches.
- Prefer morning for markets, late afternoon for viewpoints and old towns.
- If access is free but parking is paid, say it in `practical_tips`.
- Never present a paid site as free. If in doubt, describe the free part (the
  outside, the surrounding park, the panorama) and list the paid visit as a
  paid option instead.
