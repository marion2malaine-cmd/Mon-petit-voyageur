# Packing Checklist

Goal:
Provide a concise, practical, and trip-specific packing plan.

Inputs:
- Destination
- Dates or season
- Duration
- Activities
- Traveler profile

Instructions:
1. Prioritize essentials.
2. Avoid generic filler.
3. Include destination-specific reminders.
4. Separate essentials vs optional comfort items.
5. Include docs, health, and charging gear.

Output format:
- essentials
- clothing
- documents
- health_and_safety
- electronics
- optional_items
- final_reminders
